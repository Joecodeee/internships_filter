# HIGH SCHOOL INTERNSHIP & SUMMER PROGRAM GUIDE

**For Pre-College Students (Grades 9-12)**

Find the best internships, summer programs, STEM camps, and work-learn opportunities designed specifically for high school students!

## 🎯 What's Here?

This scraper finds internship and program opportunities perfect for high school students, including:

- ✅ **Summer STEM Programs** - iD Tech, Code Your Future, bootcamps
- ✅ **Paid Internships** - Real money while gaining experience
- ✅ **Research Programs** - MIT, Harvard, and other universities
- ✅ **Competitions** - Science Olympiad, TASC, math competitions
- ✅ **Work-Learn Programs** - Combined work and career exploration
- ✅ **Entry-Level Opportunities** - From platforms like The Muse

## 🚀 Quick Start (2 minutes)

### 1. Run the Scraper
```bash
python scraper_highschool.py
```

### 2. Open the Excel Files
The scraper creates professional Excel files with all opportunities:
- **high_school_internship_programs.xlsx** - Everything we found
- **high_school_verified_programs.xlsx** - Programs verified for HS students
- Multiple sheets organized by category (STEM, Paid, Summer, Research, etc.)

### 3. Find What Interests You
Each file has different program types:
- 🔬 **STEM Programs** - For science and tech lovers
- 💰 **Paid Internships** - Get paid while learning
- ☀️ **Summer Programs** - Multi-week camps and bootcamps
- 🧪 **Research Programs** - College-level research opportunities
- 🏆 **Leadership Programs** - Develop leadership skills
- 💼 **General Internships** - Various fields

## 📊 Program Types Included

### Competitive Programs (Highly Selective)
- **RSI (MIT Research Science Institute)** - 6-week STEM program, highly competitive
- **Summer Science Program** - Intensive astronomy/physics/biochemistry
- **TASP (Telluride Summer Program)** - Free program for talented students

### STEM-Focused (Great for STEM Students)
- **iD Tech Summer Camps** - Coding, robotics, AI, gaming
- **Kode With Klossy** - Free coding for girls

- **Science Olympiad Programs** - Team competition focus
- **TASC (Tech Student Association)** - Technology competition

### Paid (You Get Paid!)
- **Year Up Youth Program** - Paid internships plus training
- **Urban League Summer Employment** - Paid summer jobs
- **Boys & Girls Club Career Training** - Paid job training

### General/Broad
- **Junior Achievement (JA)** - Business/career exploration
- **Congressional Youth Leadership Council** - Politics and leadership
- **Local internships** - In your community

## 💡 Finding the Right Opportunity

### For STEM Lovers 🔬
Look for programs marked as:
- "STEM Program", "Technical", "Engineering", "Computer Science"
- Topics: Robotics, coding, physics, chemistry, biology

### For Those Who Want to Get Paid 💰
Filter for "Paid Internship" or check the description for:
- "Stipend", "Salary", "Compensation", "$"

### For Summer Only ☀️
Look for "Summer Program", "Summer Camp", "Summer Bootcamp"
- Usually 1-8 weeks in June-August

### For Leadership Lovers 🏆
Look for "Leadership Program" or descriptions with:
- "Leadership", "Skill development", "Mentoring"

## 🌍 For International Students

Many programs welcome international students! Look for descriptions mentioning:
- "International students welcome"
- "No citizenship required"
- "Open to all students"
- "F-1 students" or "Visa sponsorship"

## 🎓 Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Will these programs look good on my college application?

**A:** It varies!

**A:** Absolutely! Most programs welcome students of all academic levels:
- Some are selective (RSI, TASP)

### Q: What if I'm unsure about STEM?
**A:** Check out:
- Leadership programs

- Work-Learn programs
- General internships

### Q: Can international students apply?
**A:** Many say yes! Look for "international students welcome" in the description.

## 📋 How to Apply

When you find an opportunity you like:

1. **Visit the website** (click the URL)
2. **Read requirements** carefully
3. **Collect needed info**:
   - Your resume/CV
   - High school transcript (sometimes)
   - Essay or short answer
   - Recommendations (for competitive programs)
4. **Submit by the deadline** (don't be late!)
5. **Wait for response** (usually 2-8 weeks)

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### No programs showing up?
- Check internet connection
- Run the scraper again
- Some sources may be temporarily unavailable

### All programs look like college-level?
- The scraper filters out college-only opportunities
- We focus on HS-appropriate programs
- If something looks off, double-check program requirements

### Can't find a program you want?
- Use the URL to explore the organization's website
- Many have programs not listed here
- Google the organization name + "high school program"
