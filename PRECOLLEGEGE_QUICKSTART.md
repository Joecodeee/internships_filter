## 💡 Pro Tips

### 🏅 Apply to Multiple Programs
Don't just apply to one! Apply to 3-5 different programs:
- Hard reaches (Like RSI - probably won't get in but worth trying)
- Good fits (Medium competitive)
- Safe bets (Higher chance of acceptance)

### ⭐ Start Early
- September-October: Best time to apply
- Programs fill up first-come-first-served
- More time to prepare if rejected = can apply elsewhere

### 📝 Keep Your Resume Ready
Most programs want:
- Your name, email, phone
- Education (School, grades if relevant)
- Activities (Clubs, sports, volunteer work)
- What you want to learn
- That's it! Keep it to 1 page

### 🎯 Tailor Your Application
Each program wants:
- A short reason why you want to go
- What you hope to learn
- How it matches your interests
- Make it personal!

### ✍️ Get a Teacher to Recommend You
Most programs ask for 1-2 recommendations from:
- A teacher who knows you well
- A coach, counselor, or mentor
- Someone who can speak to your abilities
- Ask early! Teachers are busy

### 🌈 Don't Worry About "Perfection"
These programs accept:
- All grade levels (9-12)
- All interest levels (beginner to advanced)
- All backgrounds
- You don't have to be a "straight-A" student

## 📊 What to Expect from Each Type

### Summer Programs/Camps
- Usually 1-8 weeks
- June through August  
- Full-time commitment
- $$ - Ranges from free to several hundred/thousand
- 🏆 Great for college applications

### Internships (General)
- 2-12 weeks usually
- Monday-Friday (summer or school year)
- Part-time or full-time options
- 💰 Paid or unpaid (check listing)
- 📋 Real work experience

### Paid Programs
- You get PAID (like a real job!)
- Year Up: $500-1000+ for summer
- Perfect if you need money
- Great resume builder

### Research Programs
- University-based
- Very competitive
- FREE or FUNDED (they pay you!)
- 🧪 College-level experience
- 🏅 Looks amazing for college

### Competitions
- Science Olympiad, TASC
- Team-based
- School year usually
- ⭐ Wins look great on applications
- Free or low cost

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Do I have to pay?**
A: Some are free! Others charge. Check the program website.

**Q: Will this work on my college application?**
A: YES! Especially selective programs like RSI, TASP, or paid internships.

**Q: What if I don't get selected?**
A: Apply to multiple programs. There are 56+ here! You'll get into something.

**Q: Do I need experience?**
A: No! Most programs welcome beginners.

**Q: What about online programs?**
A: Several are remote/online. Look for "Remote" or "Online" in location.

**Q: Can international students apply?**
A: Many yes! Look for "International students welcome" in description.

**Q: What if I'm not good at STEM?**
A: Check Leadership Programs, Internships, or Work-Learn Programs.
